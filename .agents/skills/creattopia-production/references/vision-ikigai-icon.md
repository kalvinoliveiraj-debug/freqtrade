# Vision Board, Ikigai, and Icon Factors

Run this before scripting anything for a **new channel or brand** (skip for one-off videos on an established channel — jump straight to `aaa-vva-dream-avatar.md`).

## Step 1 — Vision Board

Four sections, each populated with images pulled from Unsplash, Pexels, Pinterest, or Google Images:

| Section | Goal |
|---|---|
| Love, Confidence, & Self-Esteem | Select visual representations of personal identity and self-confidence. |
| Wealth, Prosperity, & Career | Visualize professional milestones, financial freedom, and lifestyle goals. |
| Mind, Soul, Family, & Community | Outline future relationships, spiritual milestones, and community goals. |
| Health, Wellness, & Travel | Envision physical fitness routines, dietary lifestyles, and travel plans. |

This is a personal-clarity exercise, not a mood board for the channel's visuals — its output feeds the mission statement in Step 2, not the shot list.

## Step 2 — Ikigai (four pillars)

| Pillar | Prompt |
|---|---|
| Passion | List all topics, interests, and hobbies loved since childhood. |
| Vocation | Identify skills, technical tools, and topics the creator understands well. |
| Mission | Determine how to package that expertise to solve real-world viewer problems. |
| Profession | Map out monetizable offerings, digital products, and community platforms. |

Combine the four pillars into a single mission statement. Example:

> "The creator helps aspiring storytellers build authentic personal brands, scale their businesses, and grow closer to their faith by documenting real, transparent life changes."

Use this mission statement as the filter for every future content decision: if a video idea doesn't serve it, cut it.

## Step 3 — Icon Factors

Icon Factors are the unique elements of a creator's personality and life experience that make their content distinctive in a saturated market. Weave them into content through **subtle, consistent visual details** rather than stating them outright — e.g.:

- Consistently wearing a signature color (a blue hoodie) against a consistent complementary background (an orange-yellow sunset) to build passive visual recognition.
- Matching on-screen brand colors to the creator's/brand's existing website or product assets, so the channel visually "rhymes" with the rest of the brand.

When defining a creator's Icon Factors, capture:
1. **What makes this specific person's story/perspective non-interchangeable** (background, skills, formative experiences, quirks).
2. **A short list of recurring visual/audio signatures** (color, prop, phrase, framing choice) that can be repeated across every video without becoming a gimmick.

Feed both into the mission statement from Step 2 and into the Dream Avatar work in `aaa-vva-dream-avatar.md`.
